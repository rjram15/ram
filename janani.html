Missing You...

Janani 🌙
Nee en kooda illatha ovvoru naalum, konjam oru marii feel aaguthu...

I may not be able to see you every day, but you are always in my thoughts.

Un kooda pesura few minutes kooda, enakku romba special.

Distance namma rendu perayum far away vachirukkalam...
But it can never make my heart forget you. ❤️

Un smile, un voice, un kooda pesina moments ellame romba miss panren. nee kovo patum pothuu romba alaga irupaa ana enna chinna bayotha adichutuviyonuu 🫣
"Miles apart, but hearts always connected." 💕

Evlo distance irundhalum, en heart-la nee always close dhaan.
❤️ Enathazhagi ❤️

Unna daily paaka mudiyala... un kooda daily iruka mudiyala...

But still...
You are a beautiful part of my every day. 💗

Seekiram namma meet pannura andha beautiful day-kaaga wait panren. 🌙

I MISS YOU SO MUCH ❤️
I Love you so Much 💖
